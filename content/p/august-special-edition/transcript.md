### 1. I-10 Mobile River Bridge & Bayway Megaproject

* **Scope & Cost:** The episode opens with an overview of Alabama's proposed $3B–$5B infrastructure project to relieve severe congestion caused by the George Wallace Tunnel on Interstate 10 [[00:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L6dyOWiaei4&t=7)].
* **Engineering Design:** The plan includes a 6-lane cable-stayed bridge rising over 215 feet over the Mobile River navigation channel—making it the second-highest bridge deck over a navigable waterway in the U.S. behind the Golden Gate Bridge [[01:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L6dyOWiaei4&t=119)], [[02:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L6dyOWiaei4&t=145)].
* **Host Skepticism:** Hosts Harry Steele and Brandon Mosley voice criticism over the project's high cost, proposed driver tolls, and lack of serious consideration for alternative solutions like an additional tunnel [[03:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L6dyOWiaei4&t=201)], [[05:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L6dyOWiaei4&t=303)].
