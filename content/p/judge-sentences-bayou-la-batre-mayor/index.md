**Judge sentences ex-Bayou La Batre Mayor**  
**June 30, 2013 - Mobile Press Register**  
**Brendan Kirby**

"It's not the crime; it's the coverup." U.S. District Judge Kristi DuBose.

"This kind of behavior by anyone, but particularly on the part of a public official, has the potential to undermine the system of criminal justice," the Judge said. "It can ruin people's careers."

Attorneys argued for a longer sentence stating "The corruption was bigger than one isolated land deal, it was part of a pattern of corruption."

"At its core, this is about corruption and how it affected, all of the people of Bayou La Batre, when they were at their most vulnerable. When these millions and millions started flowing in the mayor couldn't..."

The judge responded that "What amounts to political decisions and political promises...the people could have voted him out of office."
