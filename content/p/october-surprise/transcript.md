1. Local News & Criminal Charges
Former Mobile Councilman Arrested: Former Mobile City Council member William Carroll was arrested and charged with making terroristic threats following an October 14 incident at a Hooters restaurant.
Escambia County Sting: Former State Rep. Brett Easterbrook and other local officials (including an Orange Beach public works director and Satsuma school board member) were arrested in a prostitution/human trafficking sting operation in Escambia County.
City of Mobile Cyber/Wire Fraud: The City of Mobile lost $14.7 million in a wire transfer scam/fraud during recent weather disruptions.
2. Politics & Upcoming Alabama Elections
Governor & Senate Polling: Discussion of recent polling numbers showing Republican Tommy Tuberville leading Democrat Doug Jones by around 11 to 14 points.
Statewide Constitutional Amendments:
Amendment 1 (Lieutenant Governor vacancy/pay): Host Harry Still recommended voting "No" due to concerns over expanded legislative authority to set salaries.
Amendment 2 (Consolidation of school systems): Discussion on rural school system mergers; both hosts expressed skepticism or voted "No".
Amendment 3 & 4 (School Prayer, Pledge, & Anthem): Proposals for requiring the Pledge of Allegiance, student-led prayer, and playing the National Anthem in K-12 public schools.
Local Legislative Races: Discussion on House District 65 (open seat after Easterbrook's departure) and potential shifts in Madison County seats.
3. State Infrastructure & Department of Corrections
Prison Construction Fiasco: The initial mega-prison facility in Elmore County exceeded its budget (reaching over $1.25 billion) and missed its October 15 target completion date.
Escambia Facility Contract Push: Despite Speaker Nathaniel Ledbetter urging a pause until a new administration takes office, Governor Kay Ivey's administration and the Alabama Corrections Institution Finance Authority (ACIFA) voted 6–1 to move forward with the Escambia County prison project.
DOJ Lawsuit & Prison Conditions: Discussion of ongoing federal court proceedings regarding Eighth Amendment violations in Alabama prisons, as well as alleged failures in security and backup infrastructure at Holman Correctional Facility during a recent hurricane.
4. Legal Industry & Bar Misconduct Updates
Trent Garmon Lawsuit: Suspended Alabama attorney Trent Garmon filed a federal lawsuit against the Alabama State Bar and its general counsels alleging due process violations.
Florida Bar & Morgan & Morgan: Discussion of legal advertising disputes involving non-client celebrity endorsements in law firm marketing.
Suspended Jefferson County Judge: Probate Judge Yashiba Blanchard faced formal ethics charges from the Judicial Inquiry Commission.
5. National & World News Snippets
Crypto Heist: A major hack targeting cryptocurrency exchange Binance resulted in an estimated $6.3 billion stolen, with investigators suspecting North Korean state-sponsored hackers.
UK Pubs & Crime Trends: Reports out of the UK regarding rising cocaine consumption and its impact on crime statistics.
