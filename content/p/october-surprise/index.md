In this episode, we break down the latest political shifts, key ballot measures, and legal battles shaping Alabama:

The Gubernatorial Race: New polling puts Tommy Tuberville ahead of Doug Jones (51% to 40%), while Brandon Moseley projects a 14-point margin based on traditional voting patterns. We analyze voter sentiment with the state split on its direction.

Statewide Constitutional Amendments: A close look at four critical measures on the ballot, covering lieutenant governor vacancy rules, rural school board consolidations, student-led prayer and Pledge requirements, and weekly broadcasts of the national anthem.

Prison Infrastructure & DOJ Lawsuits: Ballooning costs and blown deadlines at the $1.25 billion Elmore County site, progress on the Escambia County facility, and the ongoing federal trial over prison conditions.  Thunderdome at Holman Correction Facility during the recent Hurricane.

Legal Battles & Industry Shakeups: Trent Garmon takes on the Alabama State Bar in federal court, plus the latest on the high-profile legal advertising clash involving Morgan & Morgan. And congrats to Alex Shunnarah and Anna Nicole.

Someone stole Billions in Crypto and Glen Beck says the end is near.  Until next time.
